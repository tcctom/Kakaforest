# Modern Scandinavian Bathroom Renovation Master Plan

Comprehensive design, structural, and technical framework for the 2m x 2m Ensuite and 2m x 2.65m Main Bathroom.

---

## 📋 Project Overview & Style Identity
* **Aesthetic:** Modern Scandinavian (Minimalist, functional, natural warmth, clean lines).
* **Fixtures:** Premium chrome finishes throughout.
* **Heating:** Hydronic (warm water) underfloor heating systems.
* **Layout Goal:** Spacious, curbless walk-in showers with frameless glass panels and floating vanities.

---

## 📐 Bathroom Layout Strategies

### 1. The Ensuite (2m x 2m)
* **Wall Tiling:** **Full Tiling (Floor-to-Ceiling)**. Maximizes visual height and handles high steam concentration.
* **Floor Tiling:** Continuous large-format porcelain flowing seamlessly into the shower zone.
* **Shower Setup:** Frameless glass panel (1000mm–1200mm) with a flush, curbless entry.
* **Vanity:** Wall-hung (floating) unit mounted over fully tiled walls to maximize visible floor space.

### 2. The Main Bathroom (2m x 2.65m)
* **Wall Tiling:** **Zoned / Mixed Tiling**. Full-height (floor-to-ceiling) inside the walk-in shower zone. Half-height (~1.2m) across dry zones (toilet and vanity walls).
* **Floor Tiling:** Continuous or texturally contrasting porcelain tile.
* **Shower Setup:** Linear layout utilizing the frameless glass screen as the boundary between wet and dry zones.
* **Upper Walls:** Moisture-resistant paint in a soft Scandi tone (e.g., warm greige, sage green, or off-white).

---

## 🛠️ Material & Technical Specifications

### Tile Selection for Hydronic Underfloor Heating
* **Material:** **Porcelain tiles** are mandatory. Highly dense, conducts heat perfectly, and handles thermal expansion without cracking.
* **Slip Resistance:** R10 or R11 rating for the walk-in shower floor area to guarantee safety.
* **Grout & Joints:** Flexible grout with dedicated silicone expansion joints around the room perimeters to accommodate thermal movement.

### Linear Drainage & Floor Pitch
* **Drain Type:** **Tile-in linear drain** positioned flush against the back wall of the shower.
* **Graduation:** A flat, single-plane floor slope of **1:50 to 1:80** (approx. 1.5cm to 2cm fall per meter) starting exactly at the glass line.
* **Advantages:** Eliminates complex diagonal envelope cuts, enabling large-format tiles to run straight through the shower.

### Structural Requirements
* **Tanking (Waterproofing):** Advanced wet-room tanking membrane across the entire shower floor, climbing floor-to-ceiling on wet walls, and extending at least 1 meter into the dry floor zone.
* **Wall Reinforcement:** Heavy-duty timber nogging (blocking) must be installed between wall studs prior to tiling to support the weight of the wall-hung vanities.
* **Recessed Niches:** Framed strictly using the "tile-first" method. Niche edges should align seamlessly with the horizontal/vertical tile grout lines, using 45-degree mitred edges instead of metallic trim profiles.

## 🎨 Recommended Scandi Palettes (With Chrome Fixtures)

* **The Warm Minimalist:** Matte warm beige or greige porcelain tiles paired with a light oak or ash timber floating vanity.
* **The Classic Nordic:** Matte light grey concrete-look floor tiles, crisp matte white wall tiles, and natural wood accents.
* **The Nature-Inspired:** Soft sage green or muted olive feature tiles inside the shower zone, complemented by neutral off-white surrounding walls.

---

## 🔍 Construction Checklist for Contractors
- [ ] Recess the structural floor screed in the shower zone to accommodate the hydronic pipes and fall gradient.
- [ ] Verify the linear drain spans the exact wall-to-wall width of the shower enclosure.
- [ ] Keep hydronic heating pipes at a safe clearance distance from the drain trap assembly to prevent trap evaporation.
- [ ] Tile entirely behind the vanity position before mounting the floating cabinet unit.
- [ ] Integrate a recessed floor profile (e.g., Schluter-SHOWERPROFILE) under the frameless glass to seamlessly guide water runoff.
